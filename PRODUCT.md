# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
One person (the owner) who codes many side projects and has ideas all day: code improvements, business ideas, errands. Mostly at a desktop PC, sometimes on a phone. Occasionally a friend edits a shared list through the same link.

## Product Purpose
Never lose an idea again: capture a task or idea in seconds, find it again in the right place, and see what is in progress. Success = the owner stops using scattered .txt files.

## Positioning
Notion-level calm with a fraction of the structure: no setup, no pages, no tags. Type, Shift+Enter, done; tabs are the only structure.

## Operating Context
- Small volumes: 3 to 6 tabs, about 10 tasks per tab. Scrolling is fine; density is not a goal.
- Shared by secret link, no login. Synced live across PCs (Firestore), works offline.
- Desktop first in full width; phone is a secondary, fully usable view.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Task = title (can be long) + optional description, inline formatting (bold, lists, checklists) applied with buttons or shortcuts, not with typed syntax.
- States: À faire, En cours, Terminé. Done tasks stay in their tab, hidden unless the "Terminés" filter is on.
- Favorite (star) and pin (stays on top) per task. "Favoris" is a virtual tab.
- Tabs: created with a "+" button and renamed inline, reorderable by drag, tasks can be dropped onto a tab. No Inbox, no Archives, no hashtags, no auto-classification.
- A new task goes into the open tab (first tab when on "Tout" or "Favoris").
- Filters per tab, multi-select: Favoris, À faire, En cours, Terminés.
- Enter = new line, Shift+Enter = save. A visible send button for touch.
- Exact creation date and time shown on each task.
- Static site (GitHub Pages), vanilla JS, no build step.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: ToDo Smart.
- Visual direction pinned by the owner: soft Notion-like light mode that is easy on the eyes, plus a dark mode toggle. Airy, spacious, not compact.

## Product Principles
1. Capture speed beats everything: the input is always ready.
2. Structure comes after capture, never before.
3. Calm over clever: few elements, generous space.
4. Every action has a visible button; shortcuts are a bonus.
